Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how KeksikVerse handles information when creators use the desktop application to connect their social media accounts and publish their own content to supported platforms including TikTok and YouTube.

1. Information KeksikVerse Uses

KeksikVerse processes only the information required to provide account connection and publishing functionality requested by the user.

Depending on the connected platform, this may include account identifiers, basic profile information, authorization permissions, access tokens and refresh tokens.

2. TikTok Authorization

When a user connects a TikTok account, authentication is performed through TikTok's official authorization process.

KeksikVerse does not request, receive or store the user's TikTok password.

Users authorize KeksikVerse only for the permissions presented during the TikTok authorization process.

3. Google and YouTube Authorization

KeksikVerse uses Google OAuth 2.0 to connect a user's Google account and access YouTube functionality authorized by that user.

KeksikVerse does not request or store the user's Google password. Authentication is performed directly by Google.

For YouTube publishing, KeksikVerse may request the authorization scope required to upload videos to the user's own YouTube channel.

4. YouTube API Services

KeksikVerse uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube API Services to provide YouTube upload functionality.

When the user confirms an upload, KeksikVerse transmits the selected video and related metadata, such as the title, description, tags and publishing settings, to YouTube through the YouTube Data API.

Use of YouTube functionality is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Access and Refresh Tokens

Authorization tokens may be stored locally on the user's device so KeksikVerse can maintain an authorized connection to TikTok, Google or YouTube and perform actions requested by the user.

Tokens are used only for communication with the relevant platform APIs and are not used to request the user's platform password.

6. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through an authorized platform connection is used only to provide features requested by the user, including:

  • identifying the connected account;
  • displaying or preparing available publishing settings;
  • preparing content selected by the user;
  • uploading or publishing content after user confirmation;
  • receiving the result or status of a submitted upload when supported by the platform.

7. Video Content

KeksikVerse allows users to select their own video files for publication.

When the user confirms a publication or upload, the selected content and related publishing information are transmitted to the selected platform through its official API.

8. Data Sharing

KeksikVerse does not sell personal information.

Information is transmitted to connected third-party services only when necessary to perform functionality requested by the user, such as authentication or content publication.

9. Local Data Storage

KeksikVerse is a desktop application. Authorization information required by the application may be stored locally on the user's computer.

KeksikVerse does not require a separate KeksikVerse cloud account in order to use the local desktop publishing workflow.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their computer, operating system and local files.

10. Revoking TikTok Access

Users may revoke KeksikVerse access through the account or authorization settings provided by TikTok.

Locally stored TikTok authorization information may also be removed from the user's device.

11. Revoking Google / YouTube Access

Users may revoke KeksikVerse access to their Google account at any time through Google Account permissions.

Google Account permissions can be reviewed at: Google Account — Third-party access.

After access is revoked, KeksikVerse can no longer use the revoked Google authorization to upload videos to the user's YouTube channel.

Any locally stored Google OAuth authorization data may also be removed from the user's device.

12. Data Deletion

KeksikVerse is designed as a local desktop application. Authorization tokens and local application data stored on the user's device can be deleted by removing the locally stored authorization data or application data.

Revoking access through TikTok or Google prevents KeksikVerse from continuing to use the revoked authorization.

Content already uploaded to a third-party platform is controlled by that platform and the user's account settings on that platform.

13. Third-Party Services

KeksikVerse relies on third-party platforms and APIs, including TikTok, YouTube and Google.

Information processed directly by those services is also governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Google privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

14. Data Security

KeksikVerse is designed to limit account access to the permissions authorized by the user.

Users should protect access to their device and should revoke application authorization if they believe their account or device has been compromised.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when KeksikVerse functionality, integrations or legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be published on this page.

16. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or KeksikVerse data practices may be directed to the KeksikVerse administrator through the official KeksikVerse project website.
