Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KeksikVerse handles information when users connect social media accounts and use publishing features.

1. Information We Use

KeksikVerse may process information required to connect to third-party social media platforms, including account identifiers, authorization tokens, and permissions granted by the user.

2. TikTok Account Authorization

When a user connects a TikTok account, authentication is performed through TikTok's official authorization process. KeksikVerse does not ask for or store the user's TikTok password.

3. Access Tokens

KeksikVerse may store access tokens and refresh tokens locally on the user's device in order to maintain the connection to an authorized TikTok account and perform permitted actions through TikTok APIs.

4. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through TikTok authorization is used only to provide features requested by the user, such as connecting an account and publishing video content.

5. Data Sharing

KeksikVerse does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to TikTok or other connected platforms only as necessary to perform the requested API operations.

6. Data Storage

Authorization information used by the desktop application may be stored locally on the user's computer. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device and local files.

7. Revoking Access

Users may revoke access to connected social media accounts through the corresponding platform's account settings. Locally stored authorization information may also be removed from the user's device.

8. Third-Party Services

KeksikVerse relies on third-party services and APIs, including TikTok. Their processing of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the KeksikVerse administrator.
