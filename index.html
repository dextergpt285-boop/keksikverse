Supported platform integrations

KeksikVerse uses official platform authorization and publishing APIs. Users connect their own accounts and remain in control of every upload.

TikTok

TikTok integration

KeksikVerse integrates with TikTok using TikTok's official developer APIs. TikTok authentication is handled through TikTok's authorization flow. KeksikVerse does not request or store a user's TikTok password.

YouTube

YouTube integration

KeksikVerse integrates with YouTube using the YouTube Data API. Google OAuth 2.0 is used to authorize access to the user's own YouTube account. KeksikVerse uses authorized access to upload videos selected and confirmed by the user.

Platform policies

KeksikVerse users remain subject to the terms and privacy policies of the platforms they connect.
